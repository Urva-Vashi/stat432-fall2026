Suppose a hospital uses a random forest to predict whether a patient has heart disease. The model performs well on the training data but makes more mistakes on new patients. The hospital considers increasing the node size or changing `mtry` to improve its predictions.
How would changing these parameters affect the bias-variance trade-off, and how could out-of-bag (OOB) error help the hospital choose the best settings?

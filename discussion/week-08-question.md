Suppose a university uses a classification model to identify spam emails based on the number of links and suspicious words in each email. Two models classify the training emails correctly, but one has a wider margin (more space between the decision boundary and the closest emails), while the other has a narrower margin.

**Which model would you choose, and why? Would your choice change if the model with the wider margin incorrectly classified a few unusual emails?**

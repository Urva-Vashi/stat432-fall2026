Suppose a car dealership wants to group its vehicles based on fuel efficiency, horsepower, and weight to recommend similar cars to customers. K-means and hierarchical clustering produce different groups, and some cars end up in different clusters depending on the method. How should the dealership decide which clustering method to trust, and could standardizing the variables change which cars are grouped together?

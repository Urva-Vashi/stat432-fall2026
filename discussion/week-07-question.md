A hospital uses LDA and QDA to predict whether patients have heart disease. LDA correctly classifies 80% of patients, while QDA correctly classifies 75%, but QDA is better at identifying patients who actually have heart disease. Which model should the hospital choose, and why might overall accuracy not be the most important factor in this situation?
